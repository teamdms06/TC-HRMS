3. Leave Policy:

a) Planned Leave:

“Planned Leave” shall mean any leave that is foreseeable and applied for in advance. “Unplanned Leave” shall mean any leave arising out of unforeseen or emergency circumstances, including but not limited to illness, accident, or urgent personal matters. The Employee shall inform the HR department/ Manager/Team Leader at the earliest possible time, and in any case before the commencement of working hours on the day of absence, unless impracticable.

c) Leave During Notice Period

The Employee shall not be entitled to avail any Planned Leave during the notice period. The primary obligation of the Employee during the notice period shall be to ensure proper handover of duties and continuity of work. Any leave during the notice period shall be granted only in exceptional circumstances, at the sole discretion of the Organization, and subject to prior written approval. If the Employee fails to respond or provide a satisfactory explanation, with corresponding evidence, within 48 hours after sending such notice, the same shall be deemed as abandonment of employment. The Organization shall further be entitled to discontinue payment of salary/wages from the last working day actually attended by the Employee.

e) Any leaves taken for illness/ sickness or injury will be deducted from the employee’s sick leave entitlement.

f) In the event the Employee remains absent from work continuously for one (1) day or more on account of illness, sickness, or injury, the Employee shall submit a medical certificate issued by a registered MBBS medical practitioner within twenty-four (24) hours from the commencement of such absence, specifying the nature of the illness, sickness, or injury and certifying the Employee’s inability to attend work. If the employee failed to resume work within such a timeline, then the employee will have to provide a fresh MBBS doctor’s certificate stating the reason for the late recovery and the expected time to recover. In the event the Employee fails to resume duties within the tentative recovery period previously informed to the Organization, the Employee shall submit a fresh medical certificate issued by a registered MBBS medical practitioner specifying the reasons for the continued illness, sickness, or injury, along with the revised expected period of recovery and probable date of resumption of duties.

GUIDELINES FOR THE ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY OF THE ORGANIZATION

LEAVE RULES

1. Objective

The purpose of this Leave Policy is to regulate leave entitlement, attendance, employee welfare, operational continuity, and compliance with applicable laws. All Employees are required to adhere strictly to the leave procedures prescribed by the Organization.

2. Applicability

This Policy shall apply to all Employees of the Organization unless otherwise specifically stated in the appointment letter, employment agreement, or applicable law.

3. General Conditions Applicable to All Leaves

a) Leave cannot be claimed as a matter of right and shall always be subject to approval by the Organization.

b) Grant of leave shall depend upon work exigencies, staffing requirements, operational needs, and Management discretion.

a) Employees must apply for leave through the prescribed process and obtain prior approval before proceeding on leave, except in medical emergencies.

b) Unauthorized absence, habitual absenteeism, misuse of leave, or furnishing false information/documents shall constitute misconduct and may invite disciplinary action.

c) The Organization reserves the right to refuse, defer, curtail, revoke, or cancel any leave in the interest of business operations.

d) Employees shall remain contactable during leave wherever reasonably required by the Organization.

e) Sundays, weekly offs, public holidays, and declared holidays falling during the leave period may be counted as part of leave as per the Organization’s policy and applicable law.

f) Leave application should be sent directly to the Supervisor/ team leader; without the approval of the Supervisor/ team leader, the application will not be valid.

2. Planned Leave

a) Planned leave refers to leave taken for personal work, travel, family functions, ceremonies, vacation, or any foreseeable purpose.

b) Employees must submit leave applications in advance:

  1. Minimum seven (7) days prior notice for leave up to three (3) days.
  2. Minimum fifteen (15) days prior notice for leave exceeding three (3) days.

c) Planned leave shall be approved subject to workload, staffing availability, and Management approval.

d) Employees shall complete proper work handover before proceeding on planned leave.

e) Extension of planned leave without prior written approval shall be treated as unauthorized absence. Unplanned / Emergency Leave.

a) Unplanned leave refers to leave required due to sudden emergency, accident, urgent personal circumstances, family emergency, or unforeseen situations.

b) Employees must inform their Reporting Manager and HR Department at the earliest possible opportunity through call, message, or email.

c) The Employee may be required to submit supporting documents or justification depending upon the nature and duration of absence.

d) Frequent or habitual unplanned leave may invite scrutiny and disciplinary action if found unreasonable or abusive.

Sick Leave / Medical Leave

a) Sick leave may be granted in case of illness, sickness, injury, or medical incapacity affecting the Employee’s ability to perform duties.

b) Employees must inform the Organization immediately regarding such illness or medical condition.

c) Where absence continues for one (1) day or more, the Employee shall submit a medical certificate issued by a registered MBBS medical practitioner within twenty-four (24) hours stating the nature of illness and inability to work.

d) If the Employee fails to resume duties within the tentative recovery period informed earlier, a fresh medical certificate by a registered MBBS medical practitioner specifying the reason for delayed recovery and revised recovery period shall be submitted.

e) The Organization reserves the right to verify the authenticity of medical documents and may require additional medical examination or fitness certification before permitting resumption of duties.

f) Submission of forged or misleading medical records shall constitute serious misconduct.

Leave Without Pay (LWP)

a) Where leave balance is exhausted or leave is not approved under any eligible category, the Organization may treat such absence as Leave Without Pay (“LWP”).

b) Salary deductions for LWP shall be made proportionately as per the Organization’s payroll practices.

Right to Amend

The Organization reserves the right to modify, amend, suspend, interpret, or withdraw this Leave Policy, in whole or in part, at any time, depending upon operational requirements, Management discretion, or changes in applicable law.
