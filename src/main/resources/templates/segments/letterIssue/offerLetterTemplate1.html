LEAVE RULES

1. CASUAL / SICK LEAVE

  1. All eligible employees are entitled to 6 days of casual/sick leave in a financial year.
  2. Any unutilized leave in this category will lapse at the end of the financial year.
  3. Employee avails of sick leave; a medical certificate should also be submitted within 2 working days.
  4. Request for a Casual Leave must be made and approved by the Supervisor, at least 5 days
  5. advance of the leave taken to enable work planning and functioning during the period of absence.
  6. Casual/ Sick leave cannot be clubbed with Privilege leave

2. UNINFORMED LEAVE OR UNAPPROVED LEAVE (UL)

UL suspects misconduct and is on a temporary nonpaid status and absent from duty. Employees should be aware that UL affects entitlement to or eligibility for certain Benefits. Uninformed Leave will cause to Penalty described as follows:

  1. 1 UL will lead to a penalty of 2 days of Salary Deduction
  2. 2 UL will lead to a penalty of 4 days of Salary Deduction
  3. 3 UL may lead to Termination however the decision will be upon the Management.

3. PRIVILEGE LEAVE

All employees are eligible for 12 days of Privilege Leave in a financial year. The unused Privilege Leave can be carried forward in the next financial year while the monthly allocation will depend on the management.
